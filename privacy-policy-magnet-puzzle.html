3. About the Game

Magnet Puzzle is a logic puzzle game based on magnetic movement, planning and grid-based challenges.

The game can be played offline. Daily Challenge progress, game progress, settings and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on your device.

4. Information We May Collect

Magnet Puzzle may collect or process limited technical information, including:

  • Advertising ID,
  • device ID or other device-related identifiers,
  • crash logs and diagnostic information.

We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, precise location, contacts, photos, messages or payment information through the game.

5. How We Use Information

Technical information may be used only for purposes such as:

  • operating and maintaining the game,
  • identifying crashes, bugs and technical issues,
  • improving stability, compatibility and performance,
  • protecting the game from abuse or unauthorized activity,
  • complying with legal or platform requirements where applicable.

6. Advertising

Magnet Puzzle does not display third-party advertising.

The Advertising ID is not used by us to show third-party ads inside the game.

7. In-App Purchases

Magnet Puzzle does not offer in-app purchases.

8. Offline Gameplay and Local Storage

Core gameplay is designed to work offline. Game progress, preferences, local challenge information and other gameplay-related data may be stored directly on your device.

Removing the game or clearing its storage may remove locally stored progress and settings.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Limited technical information may be processed by trusted service providers or platform providers when necessary to distribute, operate, secure or maintain the game, including app store and crash-reporting infrastructure.
