Game Privacy Policy

Tanglewood Courier

Effective date: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MarskIT Oy (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in connection with the mobile game Tanglewood Courier.

Tanglewood Courier is designed as an offline game and does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to play.

2. Data Controller

The data controller is:

MarskIT Oy
Email: otawaram24@gmail.com

3. About the Game

Tanglewood Courier is an offline tile-matching puzzle game in which players select parcel tiles, create matching sets and clear delivery routes.

Game progress, route results, earned stars, Stamp Book progress, Daily Parcel information, settings and similar gameplay data are stored locally on the user's device.

4. Personal Data Collection

Tanglewood Courier does not require registration and does not intentionally collect personal information through the game.

We do not collect or request information such as:

  • your name,
  • email address,
  • phone number,
  • postal address,
  • precise location,
  • contacts,
  • photos or videos,
  • messages,
  • payment information,
  • advertising identifiers,
  • account credentials.

5. Offline Gameplay

Tanglewood Courier is designed to be played offline. An internet connection is not required for core gameplay.

The game does not require an online account, authentication or cloud-based profile in order to play.

6. Local Storage

Information necessary for gameplay may be stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • completed routes and unlocked progress,
  • scores and earned stars,
  • Stamp Book progress,
  • Daily Parcel and route streak information,
  • available gameplay tools and rewards,
  • game preferences and settings.

This information remains on your device and is used only to provide and preserve your gameplay experience.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its application storage may permanently remove locally stored progress, settings and other gameplay information.

7. Advertising

Tanglewood Courier does not display third-party advertising.

We do not use advertising identifiers to provide personalized or third-party advertising inside the game.

8. In-App Purchases

Tanglewood Courier does not offer in-app purchases.

No payment information is requested or processed by us through the game.

9. Accounts and Cloud Services

Tanglewood Courier does not provide user accounts, login functionality or cloud synchronization.

Gameplay progress is stored locally and is not uploaded to a personal online profile operated by us.

10. Data Sharing

Because Tanglewood Courier does not intentionally collect personal data through the game, we do not sell, rent or share personal data collected through the game with third parties.

The platform or app store through which you download the game may independently process information according to its own terms and privacy policy. Such processing is controlled by the relevant platform provider and is separate from the game itself.

11. Data Retention

Gameplay information stored by Tanglewood Courier remains locally on your device until it is deleted by the game, removed through application storage settings or erased when the application is uninstalled.

We do not maintain a remote user database containing your Tanglewood Courier gameplay profile.

12. Children's Privacy

Tanglewood Courier does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through the game.

The game does not require users to provide personal information, create an account or communicate with other users.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us in connection with the game, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action where necessary.

13. Security

Tanglewood Courier is designed to minimize privacy risks by keeping gameplay information locally on the user's device and by not requiring personal information for gameplay.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own devices and operating system.

14. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal data, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability where applicable.

Because Tanglewood Courier does not maintain user accounts or a remote database of gameplay profiles, locally stored game information can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game.

For questions concerning privacy or your rights, contact us at: otawaram24@gmail.com.
